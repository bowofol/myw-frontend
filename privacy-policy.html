Privacy Policy.

How information supports your account, item submissions and enquiries.

Draft for review — not yet approved or effective

Prepared on 17 October 2026. This document describes the current website and proposed privacy arrangements. Business and Nigerian legal review are required before it becomes a final policy. No effective date has been set.

1. Who we are and the scope of this draft

Newpage Material Managers operates this website to receive seller submissions, evaluate and negotiate purchases, resell acquired items at a public Rate, and handle buyer enquiries. This draft covers website information and related communications with our team.

The intended framework is the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) requirements, including the 2025 General Application and Implementation Directive. This draft is not a declaration of full compliance. The legal entity behind the business name and its contact address still need confirmation.

2. Information collected

  • Registration: first and last name, email address, phone number and password for a seller account. Buyer accounts are no longer offered; existing account records are retained. The database stores a password hash rather than a readable password, together with account role, status and timestamps.
  • Seller submissions: item title, description, category, condition, location, expected amount, photographs, seller account reference and submission history. Our team may contact you about evaluation, a purchase offer, drop-off and an individually agreed payment arrangement.
  • Buyer enquiries: name, phone number, message, product reference and submission date. No account is needed. Enquiries associated with existing buyer accounts retain that association; retiring buyer sign-up does not erase those records.
  • Authentication and technical data: a session identifier, limited account identity and request-security information. The server uses request IP information for abuse controls. Hosting and security providers may process connection information; their logging arrangements need confirmation before launch.
  • Direct contact: information you choose to send when contacting Newpage, including through WhatsApp. The homepage contact form prepares a WhatsApp draft; you decide whether to send it.

Please avoid uploading identity documents, unrelated personal information or photographs showing other people's private details. Required registration and enquiry fields are needed to provide those functions. You can browse published items without creating an account.

3. How information is used

We use account details to provide access and identify the relevant account holder; submission details to evaluate items and discuss possible purchases; and enquiry details to respond and coordinate buyer arrangements. Expected seller amounts and seller contact details are excluded from public product responses. Customer enquiry details are available to authorized administrators, not public visitors. Published item details and photographs are visible to visitors.

Proposed lawful bases, subject to review: account services and requested purchase or enquiry discussions may rely on a contract or steps requested before a contract; security and abuse prevention may rely on legitimate interests after assessment; specific legal duties may require processing. Consent should be used where required for a particular purpose. Browsing or creating an account is not blanket consent for unrelated uses.

4. Authentication and essential cookies

The website uses an essential session cookie to maintain sign-in and secure forms, including guest enquiry forms. Session information is stored on the server, and CSRF protection helps reject unauthorized form requests. Blocking this cookie can prevent login and form submission. You can log out explicitly or remove cookies through your browser.

The current website does not implement advertising or analytics tracking. External services may have their own practices, as described below. Any future optional tracking requires a separate review and appropriate notices and choices.

5. Sharing, providers and international processing

Authorized Newpage staff access information needed for their work. Hosting, database, image-storage and maintenance providers may need limited access to operate the service. A delivery provider may need buyer contact and delivery information when a delivery arrangement is agreed. Information may also need to be disclosed for an applicable legal requirement.

Some pages load Google Fonts and Unsplash images. Those requests disclose connection information, such as an IP address, to those services. Choosing to open WhatsApp takes you to a separate service with its own privacy terms.

Approval needed: confirm the actual providers, processing locations, access arrangements and contracts. Assess any transfer outside Nigeria and the required safeguards before production use. This draft does not claim that all data stays in Nigeria.

6. Data security

Current safeguards include password hashing, server-side authorization, protected product-image access, input checks and session protections. Production preparation includes HTTPS and secure cookies; hosting and operational controls still need verification before launch. No system can promise absolute security. Do not share your password or send passwords to the business contact.

7. Retention

Retention should reflect the purpose of collection, account and transaction needs, applicable legal requirements and unresolved disputes, rather than indefinite storage by default. The website does not currently implement an automated business-record deletion schedule.

Approval needed: establish retention periods or criteria for accounts, unsuccessful submissions, photographs, enquiries, transaction communications, logs and backups, and a deletion or anonymisation process. No fixed retention period is promised by this draft. Session expiry is separate from deletion of business records.

8. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable conditions and exceptions, you may request information about processing, access to your data, correction, erasure or restriction; object to processing; request portability; and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Applicable rights concerning solely automated decisions also remain available. Item evaluation and publishing are handled by our team.

You can contact Newpage to raise a request or concern. Reasonable identity verification may be needed before disclosing or changing information. Requests must be assessed under applicable legal timelines; this draft does not promise automatic deletion of records that must lawfully be retained. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, independently of contacting us.

For background, see the NDPC's explanation of privacy principles and rights. That notice describes NDPC's own processing, not Newpage's practices.

9. Privacy contact and matters awaiting approval

Contact Newpage Material Managers on WhatsApp at 08037011088 (+2348037011088) and identify your message as a privacy request. This is the confirmed general business contact, not a separately designated data protection officer. Ask for an appropriate channel before sending sensitive material.

Before approval, confirm the legal business identity and address, privacy contact responsibilities, treatment of minors, lawful-basis assessments, provider and transfer arrangements, retention schedule and a working rights-request process. The final notice must reflect those decisions. Related commercial arrangements are described in the draft Terms & Conditions.
