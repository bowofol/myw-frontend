1. How Newpage operates

Newpage Material Managers evaluates reusable and secondhand items submitted by sellers and may negotiate to purchase them. Newpage then resells acquired items through its Marketplace at a public Rate set by its team. Buyer enquiries go to Newpage, which coordinates the transaction.

The website is not a direct seller-to-buyer payment platform. Submitting an item does not guarantee that Newpage will purchase it.

2. Seller eligibility and lawful ownership

Sellers should have legal capacity to enter the proposed transaction and be the lawful owner of the item, or have authority from the owner to sell it. Do not submit stolen, unlawful or misrepresented items. Describe condition, defects and any relevant ownership restrictions accurately, and use photographs you are entitled to provide.

Keep account information accurate and protect your login credentials. The eligibility and verification arrangements, including any treatment of minors, require business and legal approval; this draft does not invent an age threshold or identity-document requirement.

3. Item submissions, evaluations and purchase offers

Register as a seller, submit item details and clear photographs, and provide your expected amount. That amount is a private starting point for discussion, not an accepted purchase offer or the public resale Rate. Newpage reviews and evaluates the submission and may contact you to discuss condition, suitability and a negotiated purchase amount.

A submission is initially Pending Review. Submission, review or a discussion does not itself guarantee purchase. Any agreed purchase amount and relevant arrangements should be recorded directly with Newpage before proceeding.

4. Seller drop-off and payment arrangements

The seller arranges to bring the item to Newpage. Confirm the drop-off location and practical arrangements with the team before travelling; this website does not promise seller collection or provide an approved drop-off address.

The seller and Newpage agree payment method and timing individually. This draft does not promise cash payment, instant payment, payment on submission or any fixed payment deadline. Confirm the agreed amount, method and timing directly with the team for your transaction.

5. Purchase and ownership transfer

Newpage purchases items before its team publishes them for resale. Publication is an administrative action; it is not evidence of a particular seller-payment or ownership-transfer event.

Business approval needed: the precise point at which ownership and risk transfer, any conditions of handover and the transaction records required have not been specified. These must be reviewed and documented in the relevant purchase arrangements. This draft does not set transfer to occur automatically on submission, drop-off or payment.

6. Public resale Rates, availability and enquiries

Newpage sets the public resale Rate for acquired items. The public Rate is separate from the amount privately negotiated with the original seller. Buyers can browse and enquire without an account. Use the website enquiry form or WhatsApp on the product page to contact Newpage. Website enquiries are sent privately to our team; WhatsApp opens a draft that you choose to send.

An enquiry is a request for discussion, not confirmation of a completed purchase or a reservation. Confirm current availability, condition, the agreed Rate and the purchase arrangements with Newpage. The website does not currently take online payments. Sold items are removed from the active Marketplace.

7. Buyer pickup, delivery and charges

Buyers can collect purchased items or arrange delivery with Newpage. Agree the collection or delivery arrangements with the team before proceeding.

Delivery charges depend on the buyer's location and the agreed arrangement. The charge must be communicated before purchase. This draft does not promise free delivery, a fixed delivery fee, a particular delivery area or a delivery deadline.

8. Refunds, warranties and other approval matters

Business and legal approval needed: refund and return arrangements, warranties and handling of faulty or misdescribed items have not been confirmed. No refund or warranty policy is created by this draft. Request the applicable arrangements before a transaction is agreed.

The final terms also need the confirmed legal business identity and contact address, an approved ownership-transfer process, seller eligibility rules and an effective date. This draft does not create a waiver of applicable statutory rights.

Account, submission and enquiry information is described in the draft Privacy Policy.

9. Questions, disputes and contact

Contact Newpage Material Managers on WhatsApp at 08037011088 (+2348037011088) with the item reference and a clear description of your concern. Keep the relevant agreed transaction details. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

The team can discuss the concern and proposed next steps with you. A formal complaints process and escalation arrangements require approval. This draft does not impose a dispute deadline, exclusive court, mandatory arbitration or a limitation of liability.
