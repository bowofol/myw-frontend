02 / THE NEWPAGE WAY

A better way
to pass it on.

You sell to Newpage. Buyers buy from Newpage.
One team, from the first review to the next chapter.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you have.

    Create a seller account, then submit item details, clear photos and your private expected amount. Submission does not guarantee purchase.

  2. 02

    We review. We talk.

    Newpage evaluates your item and discusses a possible purchase with you. Your expected amount is a starting point for negotiation.

  3. 03

    Agree on its next move.

    If a purchase is agreed, you arrange to bring the item to Newpage. Payment method and timing are agreed individually.

  4. 04

    A new chapter begins.

    Newpage publishes acquired items at a public Rate. Buyers enquire with our team, then collect or arrange delivery. Delivery charges depend on location and the agreed arrangement and are communicated before purchase.
